# Mission Statement

## Product Vision
A SaaS platform for independent car showroom owners - each showroom gets a dedicated app instance with customer marketplace, inventory management, and investor dashboard tailored to their business model.

## Core Purpose
To empower individual car showroom owners to digitize their operations by providing:
1. A **customer marketplace** (buy/sell cars locally)
2. **Inventory management** (internal tracking and automation)
3. **Investor dashboards** (portfolio & profit/loss tracking)

Each showroom owner can deploy and manage their own independent instance of the app.

## Deployment Model: Single-Tenant SaaS

Each showroom owner gets their **own independent deployment**:
- Separate database & API instance
- Isolated customer data
- Showroom-specific branding & configuration
- Independent pricing/commission rules
- Simple "white-label" setup for quick deployment

---

## Key Stakeholders & Their Needs

### 1. **Showroom Owner** (Primary Customer)
- **Need**: Easy way to manage their car business online
- **Need**: Control over investor relationships and profit sharing
- **Need**: Automation of manual inventory processes
- **Goal**: Reduce operational overhead and increase transparency

### 2. **Customers** (Using the Showroom App)
- **Need**: Browse cars listed by THIS showroom
- **Need**: Simple car-selling process
- **Need**: Upload photos and specifications
- **Goal**: Buy/sell cars easily through showroom's app

### 3. **Showroom Staff**
- **Need**: Inventory management dashboard
- **Need**: Customer inquiry tracking
- **Need**: Automated reporting
- **Goal**: Streamline daily operations

### 4. **Investors** (Shareholders in the Showroom)
- **Need**: See their portfolio of cars within THIS showroom
- **Need**: Ownership percentage & profit/loss tracking
- **Need**: Real-time investment performance
- **Goal**: Monitor their stake transparently

---

## Success Criteria

**For Showroom Owners (Sales Metrics)**
- ✓ Can be onboarded and deploy in < 1 day
- ✓ Customers can browse & list cars in < 2 minutes
- ✓ Staff can manage inventory without paper trails
- ✓ Investors see real-time P&L on their investments
- ✓ Works on mobile & web

**For You (Product Metrics)**
- ✓ One codebase deployable to multiple showrooms
- ✓ Easy to update all instances simultaneously
- ✓ Clear pricing/licensing model per showroom
- ✓ Minimal manual work to onboard new customers

---

## Revenue Model

**Per-Showroom Licensing** (Suggested)
- Monthly subscription per showroom ($500-2000 depending on features)
- OR commission on transactions (5-10%)
- OR hybrid (base fee + commission)

Each showroom pays once, gets their own instance with full features.
